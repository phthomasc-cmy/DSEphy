Heat Capacity & Specific Heat Capacity 熱容量與比熱容量

Understanding why we need "per unit mass" for fair comparisons 理解為何比較不同物質時必須以「單位質量」為基準

1. Concept Comparison: C vs. c 1. 概念對比：熱容量 vs. 比熱容量

Heat Capacity (C) 熱容量 (C)

C = E / ΔT

Unit: J °C⁻¹ (or J K⁻¹) 單位： J °C⁻¹（或 J K⁻¹）

Definition: The energy needed to raise the temperature of the entire object by 1 °C. 定義： 使整個物體升高 1 °C 所需吸收的能量。

Depends on: Both the mass and the material. (Extrinsic property) 取決於： 物體的質量與材質。（外延性質）

Specific Heat Capacity (c) 比熱容量 (c)

c = C / m = E / (m · ΔT)

Unit: J kg⁻¹ °C⁻¹ 單位： J kg⁻¹ °C⁻¹

Definition: The energy needed to raise the temperature of 1 kg of the material by 1 °C. 定義： 使 1 kg 該物質升高 1 °C 所需吸收的能量。

Depends on: The material only. (Intrinsic property, independent of mass!) 取決於： 僅取決於物質種類。（內稟性質，與質量無關！）

2. Why Measure "Per Unit Mass"? (Fair Comparison) 2. 為什麼比較不同材料時必須以「單位質量」計算？（公平比較）

Imagine comparing a tiny spoonful of water with an enormous 100 kg block of aluminium. The huge aluminium block requires far more heat to warm up, so its Heat Capacity (C) is larger. Does that mean aluminium is better at storing heat than water? No! That is unfair because the masses are completely different. 想像一下：用一小茶匙的水與一整塊重達 100 kg 的鋁磚相比。這塊巨大的鋁磚需要極多能量才能升溫，因此它的熱容量 (C) 遠高於茶匙裡的水。這是否意味著「鋁比水更能儲存熱能」？並非如此！這是因為兩者質量懸殊，比較並不公平。

420 J °C⁻¹

Heat Capacity C = m · c 熱容量 C = m · c

Aluminium (鋁) 鋁 (Aluminium)

4500 J °C⁻¹

3. Interactive Textbook Example (Aluminium Block) 3. 課本範例重現與計算機（鋁塊）

From the image: We need 45 kJ (45,000 J) to heat up a 5 kg aluminium block from 20 °C to 30 °C. 對應圖中範例：我們需要 45 kJ (45,000 J) 將一塊 5 kg 的鋁塊由 20 °C 加熱至 30 °C。

Step 1: Calculate Heat Capacity of the block (C) 步驟 1：計算該鋁塊的熱容量 (C)

ΔT = 30 °C - 20 °C = 10 °C
C = E / ΔT = 45 000 J / 10 °C = 4500 J °C⁻¹

Step 2: Eliminate mass dependence to find Specific Heat Capacity (c) 步驟 2：消除質量影響，得出鋁的比熱容量 (c)

c = C / m = 4500 J °C⁻¹ / 5 kg = 900 J kg⁻¹ °C⁻¹

👉 Meaning: Exactly 900 J is required to heat up 1 kg of aluminium by 1 °C. 👉 物理意義：每將 1 kg 的鋁升高 1 °C，恰好需要吸收 900 J 的能量。
