Two of the blocks are made of copper and the other of lead. The masses of X and Z are twice that of Y (mX = 2mY, mZ = 2mY).

【解題提示】
1. 應用比熱容量公式：Pt = mcΔT ⇒ 斜率 (slope) = P / (mc)。
2. 比較金屬 Y 與 Z：
