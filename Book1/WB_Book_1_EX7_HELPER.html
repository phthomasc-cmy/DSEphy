Question 1 (P.109 Example 3.1): Heating Curve of a Substance

Suppose 500 g (0.5 kg) of a substance is heated uniformly at a constant rate of 300 W and changes from a solid to a gas.
The specific heat capacity of the solid state is csolid = 7700 J kg⁻¹ °C⁻¹.
Step 1 (a)(i) Find the melting point T₁ (°C)

The solid state is heated from t = 0 to t = 6 min to reach its melting point T₁.

Melting Point T₁ = °C
【解題提示】
1. 固體受熱時間為 0 至 6 分鐘：t = 6 min = 6 × 60 = 360 s。
2. 輸入電能：E = Pt = 300 × 360 = 108 000 J。
3. 應用吸熱公式：Pt = mcsolidΔT ⇒ 108 000 = 0.5 × 7700 × (T₁ − 0)。
4. 解得 T₁ = 108 000 / 3850 ≈ 28.05 °C (約 28.1 °C)。
Step 2 (a)(ii) Find the specific latent heat of fusion ℓf (kJ kg⁻¹)

Melting occurs along the first horizontal plateau (from t = 6 min to 15 min).

ℓf = kJ kg⁻¹
【解題提示】
1. 熔化階段時間：Δt = 15 − 6 = 9 min = 9 × 60 = 540 s。
2. 熔化吸收能量：E = PΔt = 300 × 540 = 162 000 J = 162 kJ。
3. 應用潛熱公式：Pt = mℓf ⇒ ℓf = 162 000 / 0.5 = 324 000 J kg⁻¹ = 324 kJ kg⁻¹。
Step 3 (a)(iii) Find the specific latent heat of vaporization ℓv (kJ kg⁻¹)

Boiling occurs along the second horizontal plateau (from t = 22 min to 35 min).

ℓv = kJ kg⁻¹
【解題提示】
1. 汽化階段時間：Δt = 35 − 22 = 13 min = 13 × 60 = 780 s。
2. 汽化吸收能量：E = PΔt = 300 × 780 = 234 000 J = 234 kJ。
3. 應用公式：Pt = mℓv ⇒ ℓv = 234 000 / 0.5 = 468 000 J kg⁻¹ = 468 kJ kg⁻¹。
Step 4 (b) Find the specific heat capacity in liquid state cliquid (J kg⁻¹ °C⁻¹)

If the boiling point T₂ is 80 °C, and the liquid heats up between t = 15 min and 22 min.

cliquid = J kg⁻¹ °C⁻¹
【解題提示】
1. 液體升溫時間：Δt = 22 − 15 = 7 min = 7 × 60 = 420 s。
2. 提供能量：E = Pt = 300 × 420 = 126 000 J。
3. 溫度變化量：ΔT = 80 − 28.05 = 51.95 °C（若以 28.1 °C 計算則為 51.9 °C）。
4. 應用公式：Pt = mcΔT ⇒ 126 000 = 0.5 × c × 51.95 ⇒ c ≈ 4850 J kg⁻¹ °C⁻¹。

Question 2 (P.112 Example 3.2): Latent Heat of Fusion of Ice Experiment

To measure ℓf of ice, Sam puts 0 °C crushed ice in both funnels (Experimental setup with heater turned on vs. Control setup without power) and switches on the immersion heater after dripping rates equalize.
Data: Mass in beaker A (experimental) = 0.084 kg; Mass in beaker B (control) = 0.013 kg.
Initial joulemeter reading = 20 485 J; Final joulemeter reading = 41 785 J.
Step 1 (a) Calculate the specific latent heat of fusion ℓf (kJ kg⁻¹)

Determine net electrical energy supplied and net mass of ice melted solely by the heater.

ℓf = kJ kg⁻¹
【解題提示】
1. 電能提供：E = 41 785 − 20 485 = 21 300 J。
2. 僅由加熱器熔化的淨冰質量：m = mA − mB = 0.084 − 0.013 = 0.071 kg。
3. 應用公式：ℓf = E / m = 21 300 / 0.071 = 300 000 J kg⁻¹ = 300 kJ kg⁻¹。
Step 2 (b) Systematic Error Analysis (Larger or Smaller?)

Predict whether the experimental value would be larger or smaller than the standard value in each case:

(i) Ice cubes are used instead of crushed ice:
(ii) Less ice in the control than the experimental apparatus:
(iii) There is no control apparatus used:
(iv) The heating coil is not immersed into the ice completely:
【系統誤差分析提示】
公式：ℓf = E / m。
• (i) 冰塊接觸不良：冰粒間有空隙，熱量向周圍散失，熔化相同冰所需 E 偏大（overestimated）⇒ ℓf Larger。
• (ii) 對照組冰過少：對照組熔化的水偏少，扣除的 mB 偏小，導致計算淨質量 m 偏大 ⇒ ℓf Smaller。

• (iv) 加熱器外露：部分熱量直接散失至空氣中，計入電表的 E 比真正融冰的能量多 ⇒ ℓf Larger。

Question 3 (P.113 Example 3.3): Latent Heat of Vaporization Experiment

Mandy uses a kilowatt-hour meter to measure the energy consumed to boil water.
Initial mass of water = 0.550 kg  |  Final mass of water = 0.374 kg
Meter specification = 300 rev / kWh  |  Revolutions counted = 38 rev
Step 1 (a) Total electrical energy consumed (kJ)
Total energy E = kJ
【解題提示】
1. 1 kWh = 1000 W × 3600 s = 3.6 × 10⁶ J = 3600 kJ。
2. 每千瓦小時 300 轉，轉動 38 轉耗電：E = (38 / 300) × 3.6 × 10⁶ J = 456 000 J = 456 kJ。
Step 2 (b) Calculate the specific latent heat of vaporization ℓv (kJ kg⁻¹)
ℓv = kJ kg⁻¹
【解題提示】
1. 汽化（蒸發）的水質量：m = 0.550 − 0.374 = 0.176 kg。
2. 應用公式：E = mℓv ⇒ 456 000 = 0.176 × ℓv。
3. ℓv = 456 000 / 0.176 ≈ 2.59 × 10⁶ J kg⁻¹ = 2590 kJ kg⁻¹。

(ii) Replacing the glass beaker with a polystyrene cup:

【改進措施分析】
• (i) 浸沒發熱部分：Yes。防止發熱絲直接把熱量散失到空氣中。
• (ii) 改用發泡膠杯：Yes。發泡膠是優良絕熱體，減少杯壁散熱。

Question 4 (P.116 Example 3.4): Evaporative Cooler

An evaporative cooler cools air through the evaporation of water in a room measuring 15 m × 12 m × 5 m filled with air at 27 °C.
The cooler operates for 20 minutes with a useful power output of 5.8 kW.
Take: Air density = 1.2 kg m⁻³, cair = 1.005 kJ kg⁻¹ °C⁻¹, and ℓv = 2260 kJ kg⁻¹.
Step 1 (a) Total energy taken away from room air (J)
E = J
【解題提示】
1. 功率 P = 5.8 kW = 5800 W。
2. 時間 t = 20 min = 20 × 60 = 1200 s。
3. 公式：E = Pt = 5.8 × 10³ × 1200 = 6.96 × 10⁶ J (或 6960 kJ)。
Step 2 (b) Mass of water evaporated (kg)
Mass m = kg
【解題提示】
1. 應用公式：E = mℓv。
2. 6.96 × 10⁶ J = m × (2260 × 10³ J kg⁻¹)。
3. m = 6.96 × 10⁶ / 2.26 × 10⁶ ≈ 3.08 kg (約 3.1 kg)。
Step 3 (c) Resulting temperature in the room (°C)
Room temperature T = °C
【解題提示】
1. 房間空氣質量：mair = 體積 × 密度 = (15 × 12 × 5) × 1.2 = 900 × 1.2 = 1080 kg。
2. 空氣熱容量：mc = 1080 × 1005 J °C⁻¹ = 1 085 400 J °C⁻¹。
3. 降溫幅度：ΔT = E / (mc) = 6.96 × 10⁶ / 1 085 400 ≈ 6.41 °C。
4. 最終室溫：T = 27 − 6.41 = 20.59 °C ≈ 20.6 °C。
