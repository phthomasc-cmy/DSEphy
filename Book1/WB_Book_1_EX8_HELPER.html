Question 1: Melting Ice to Steam

How much energy is required to completely convert 200 g (0.2 kg) of melting ice at 0 °C to steam at 100 °C?

【階段計算提示】
1. E₁ (熔化): E₁ = mℓf = 0.2 kg × 334 kJ kg⁻¹ = 66.8 kJ。
2. E₂ (升溫): E₂ = mcΔT = 0.2 kg × 4.2 kJ kg⁻¹ °C⁻¹ × (100 − 0) °C = 84 kJ。
3. E₃ (汽化): E₃ = mℓv = 0.2 kg × 2260 kJ kg⁻¹ = 452 kJ。

Total Energy E = kJ
【解題提示】
Etotal = E₁ + E₂ + E₃ = 66.8 + 84 + 452 = 602.8 kJ (即 602 800 J)。

Question 2: Melting a Piece of Gold

Properties of gold: Melting point = 1060 °C, Specific heat capacity c = 130 J kg⁻¹ °C⁻¹, Latent heat of fusion ℓf = 6700 J kg⁻¹.
Find the minimum energy required to completely melt 0.6 kg of gold originally at 20 °C.
Step 1 Calculate energy required to heat solid gold from 20 °C to 1060 °C (J)
E₁ = J
【解題提示】
1. 溫差：ΔT = 1060 − 20 = 1040 °C。
2. 應用比熱容量公式：E₁ = mcΔT = 0.6 kg × 130 J kg⁻¹ °C⁻¹ × 1040 °C = 81 120 J。
Step 2 Calculate latent heat required to melt the gold at 1060 °C (J)
E₂ = J
【解題提示】
熔化所需潛熱：E₂ = mℓf = 0.6 kg × 6700 J kg⁻¹ = 4020 J。
Step 3 Calculate total minimum energy required E (J)
Total E = J
【解題提示】
總能量：E = E₁ + E₂ = 81 120 + 4020 = 85 140 J。

Question 3: Cup of Water in a Freezer

A cup of water of mass 0.4 kg (volume 400 cm³) initially at 25 °C is put into a freezer and turns into ice at −18 °C.
Take: cwater = 4.2 kJ kg⁻¹ °C⁻¹, ℓf = 334 kJ kg⁻¹, cice = 2.1 kJ kg⁻¹ °C⁻¹.
Step 1 (a) Calculate the total energy released (kJ)

Divide the cooling process into 3 stages: cooling water to 0 °C, freezing water to ice at 0 °C, and cooling ice to −18 °C.

Total Energy Released E = kJ
【3 個階段放熱提示】
1. 水由 25 °C 降至 0 °C: 0.4 × 4.2 × (25 − 0) = 42 kJ。
2. 0 °C 水凝固成 0 °C 冰: 0.4 × 334 = 133.6 kJ。
3. 0 °C 冰降至 −18 °C: 0.4 × 2.1 × [0 − (−18)] = 0.4 × 2.1 × 18 = 15.12 kJ。
• 總放熱 E = 42 + 133.6 + 15.12 = 190.72 kJ ≈ 191 kJ (即 190 720 J)。
Step 2 (b) Estimate average rate of energy released (power in W)

The whole cooling process takes 900 s (15 minutes).

Average Power P = W
【解題提示】
1. 應用功率公式：E = Pt ⇒ P = E / t。
2. 代入焦耳單位：P = 190 720 J / 900 s ≈ 211.9 W ≈ 212 W (若用 191 000 J 則約為 212.2 W)。

Question 4: Melting Ice Mixed with Hot Water

A block of melting ice of 500 g (0.5 kg) at 0 °C is placed into a bucket containing 2 kg of water at 80 °C.
Estimate the final equilibrium temperature of the mixture. (Take cwater = 4.2 kJ kg⁻¹ °C⁻¹, ℓf = 334 kJ kg⁻¹).
Step 1 Set up heat exchange equation & solve for T (°C)

Energy absorbed by melting ice and warming up = Energy released by cooling hot water.

Final Temperature T = °C
【熱平衡方程提示】
1. 冰吸收能量：Eabs = miceℓf + micec(T − 0)
  = 0.5 × 334 + 0.5 × 4.2 × T = 167 + 2.1T (kJ)。
2. 熱水放出能量：Erel = mwaterc(80 − T)
  = 2 × 4.2 × (80 − T) = 8.4 × (80 − T) = 672 − 8.4T (kJ)。
3. 列出等式：167 + 2.1T = 672 − 8.4T
  10.5T = 505 ⇒ T = 505 / 10.5 ≈ 48.1 °C。

Question 5: Hot Copper Block in Melting Ice

A copper block of mass 1.7 kg at 300 °C is immersed into 0.3 kg of melting ice at 0 °C.
Given: Melting point of copper = 1080 °C, ccopper = 390 J kg⁻¹ °C⁻¹, ℓf = 3.34 × 10⁵ J kg⁻¹, cwater = 4200 J kg⁻¹ °C⁻¹.
Estimate the final equilibrium temperature of the copper block.
Step 1 Solve for final equilibrium temperature T (°C)

Energy released by cooling copper = Energy absorbed by melting ice and warming resulting water.

Final Temperature T = °C
【解題提示】
1. 銅塊降溫放熱：mCucCu(300 − T) = 1.7 × 390 × (300 − T) = 663 × (300 − T) = 198 900 − 663T。
2. 冰吸熱融化並升溫：miceℓf + micecwater(T − 0)
  = 0.3 × 334 000 + 0.3 × 4200 × T = 100 200 + 1260T。
3. 能量守恆：198 900 − 663T = 100 200 + 1260T
  1923T = 98 700 ⇒ T = 98 700 / 1923 ≈ 51.3 °C。

Question 6: Steaming a Fish

Steam at 120 °C is used to cook a fish of mass 600 g initially at 15 °C. The final temperature of the fish is 88 °C.
Given: Heat capacity of fish Cfish = 2100 J °C⁻¹, csteam = 1900 J kg⁻¹ °C⁻¹, ℓv = 2.26 × 10⁶ J kg⁻¹, cwater = 4200 J kg⁻¹ °C⁻¹.
Step 1 Calculate energy absorbed by the fish (J)

The fish temperature rises from 15 °C to 88 °C (using its heat capacity Cfish).

Efish = J
【解題提示】
1. 溫升：ΔT = 88 − 15 = 73 °C。
2. 已知魚的熱容量 Cfish = 2100 J °C⁻¹ (注意是熱容量 C，不需再乘質量)。
3. 吸收熱量：E = CfishΔT = 2100 × 73 = 153 300 J。
Step 2 Estimate the mass of steam used (kg)

Steam cools from 120 °C → 100 °C, condenses into water at 100 °C, and condensed water cools from 100 °C → 88 °C.

Mass of steam msteam = kg
【蒸汽三段放熱提示】
設所需蒸汽質量為 m：
1. 蒸汽由 120 °C 降至 100 °C: m × 1900 × (120 − 100) = 38 000m J。
2. 蒸汽在 100 °C 凝結: m × (2.26 × 10⁶) = 2 260 000m J。
3. 凝結水由 100 °C 降至 88 °C: m × 4200 × (100 − 88) = 50 400m J。
• 蒸汽總放熱 = (38 000 + 2 260 000 + 50 400)m = 2 348 400m J。
• 列方程：2 348 400m = 153 300 ⇒ m ≈ 0.0653 kg (約 65.3 g)。
