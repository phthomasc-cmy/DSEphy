Graphs of Power (P) & Energy (E)

物理圖形關係：功率 (P) 與 能量 (E)

💡 Core Physical Principle / 核心物理概念
Power P =
ΔE Δt
= Slope of E–t Graph
Rate of energy transfer = Tangent slope of energy–time graph | 能量傳遞率 = 能量–時間圖線之切線斜率
1. Constant Power (P = const)
1. 恆定功率（P = 常數）
  • • E–t is a straight line through origin (0, 0).
  • • Higher Power → Steeper slope.
  • • E–t 圖為通過原點 (0, 0) 之直線。
  • • 功率愈大 → 斜率愈大（圖線愈陡峭）。
2. Decreasing Power (P decreases)
2. 漸減功率（P 隨時間下降）
  • • Tangent slope gets flatter and flatter.
  • • As P → 0, curve becomes horizontal.
  • • E–t 切線斜率愈變愈平緩。
  • • 當 P → 0，圖線趨於水平線。
3. Increasing Power (P increases)
3. 漸增功率（P 隨時間上升）
  • • Tangent slope gets steeper and steeper.
  • • E–t curves upwards (concave up).
  • • E–t 切線斜率愈變愈陡峭。
  • • E–t 圖線向上彎曲（凹向上）。
