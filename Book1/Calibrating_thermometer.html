Why Linearity Matters in Calibration

In a liquid-in-glass thermometer, the length ℓ varies linearly with temperature θ. Because the graph is a straight line, two fixed points are sufficient to construct the entire scale.

In contrast, many electronic components—such as a thermistor (Fig. 1.8 in textbook)—exhibit a non-linear relationship: resistance (R) drops rapidly at lower temperatures and flattens out at higher temperatures.

Key Principles:
• Linear Sensor: 2 calibration points determine the entire scale.
• Non-Linear Sensor: 2 points are insufficient; a multi-point calibration curve is necessary.
