Question 1 (Multiple Choice)
Answer: C
On a sunny day, a wall of 5.0 m2 absorbs energy at a rate of 350 W on every 1 m2. Find the energy absorbed by the wall in 1.5 hours.
題目：晴天時，一幅面積為 5.0 m2 的牆壁每 1 m2 以 350 W 的速率吸收能量。求該牆在 1.5 小時內吸收的能量。
• Total Rate of Energy (Power, P) = 350 W m⁻² × 5.0 m² = 1750 W (J s⁻¹)
• Time (t) = 1.5 h = 1.5 × 3600 s = 5400 s
• Energy Absorbed (E) = P × t = 1750 W × 5400 s = 9,450,000 J = 9.45 MJ
解析 功率為每秒轉移的能量 (P = E / t)。計算關鍵是時間單位換算：必須先把 1.5 小時換算成秒 (1.5 × 3600 = 5400 s)，再代入 E = P × t 計算焦耳，最後轉成兆焦耳 (9.45 MJ)。
Question 2 (Multiple Choice)
Answer: B
16500 J of energy is supplied to a metal block of mass 0.6 kg and its rise in temperature is 57 °C. The specific heat capacity of the metal is:
題目：將 16500 J 的能量提供給質量為 0.6 kg 的金屬塊，其溫度上升了 57 °C。該金屬的比熱容量為：
A. 16500 × 0.657 J kg-1 °C-1
B. 1650057 × 0.6 J kg-1 °C-1  ✓
C. 16500 × 570.6 J kg-1 °C-1
D. [165000.6 − 57] J kg-1 °C-1
Formula: E = m · c · ΔT
Rearranging for specific heat capacity (c):
c = Em × ΔT = 1650057 × 0.6 J kg-1 °C-1
解析 公式為 E = mcΔT。求比熱容量 c 時，將質量 m (0.6 kg) 與溫度變化 ΔT (57 °C) 移至等號另一邊的分母相乘，故選項 B 正確。
Question 3 (Multiple Choice)
Answer: C
An equal amount of energy is supplied to each of the following substances. Which one of them will have the smallest rise in temperature?
題目：將相同分量的能量提供給下列各物質。哪一種物質的溫度升幅最小？
Substance 物質 Mass 質量 (kg) Specific Heat Capacity 比熱容量 (J kg-1 °C-1) Total Heat Capacity C = m × c (J °C-1)
A. P142001 × 4200 = 4200
B. Q223002 × 2300 = 4600
C. R322003 × 2200 = 6600 (Largest C) ✓
D. S49004 × 900 = 3600
Formula: ΔT = E / (m · c) = E / C (Heat Capacity)
• To get the SMALLEST ΔT for a given E, we require the LARGEST Heat Capacity (C = m × c).
• Substance R has the largest C (6600 J °C⁻¹), so it requires the most energy per 1 °C increase, meaning it will experience the smallest temperature rise.
解析 由 ΔT = E / (mc) 可知，在能量 E 相同時，總熱容量 C = mc 越大的物質，溫度升幅 ΔT 越小。物質 R 的總熱容量最高 (6600 J °C⁻¹)，因此升溫幅度最小。
Question 4 (Multiple Choice)
Answer: C [(2) and (3) only]
The apparatus on the right is used to find the specific heat capacity of a liquid. Which of the following can improve the accuracy of the experiment?
  1. Taking the final temperature of the liquid immediately after switching off the power supply.
  2. Covering the cup with a lid.
  3. Stirring the liquid throughout the experiment.
題目：右圖裝置用於量度液體的比熱容量。下列哪項／些措施可以提高實驗的準確度？
  1. 關掉電源後立即讀取液體的最後溫度。
  2. 為杯子加上蓋子。
  3. 在整個實驗過程中不斷攪拌液體。
• Statement (1) is WRONG: The immersion heater retains heat even after being powered off. You must continue stirring until the thermometer reaches the maximum steady reading.
• Statement (2) is CORRECT: Adding a lid prevents heat loss via convection and evaporation.
• Statement (3) is CORRECT: Continuous stirring distributes thermal energy evenly throughout the liquid.
解析 (1) 錯誤：發熱器斷電後仍存有餘熱，應持續攪拌直至溫度計達到最高讀數；
(2) 正確：加蓋能有效減少蒸發及對流流失熱量；
(3) 正確：攪拌可確保整杯液體溫度均勻。故只有 (2) 和 (3) 正確。
Question 5 (Structured Question)
4 Marks Total
[Scenario]: A solar water heating system has a solar panel of area 0.64 m2. The panel captures 55% of the solar energy it receives, and uses the energy to heat water. Assume sunlight delivers energy onto the Earth's surface at 1 kW m-2.
Given that 6.3 × 105 J of energy is required to heat up a bathtub of water.
【背景資料】：一個太陽能熱水系統配有面積為 0.64 m2 的太陽能板。該板能吸收其接收到的 55% 太陽能，並將能量用於加熱水。假設陽光照射到地球表面的能量速率為 1 kW m-2。
已知加熱一浴缸水需要 6.3 × 105 J 的能量。

a) How many minutes will it take for the heating process? [3 marks]

(a) 加熱過程需要多少分鐘？ [3分]
1. Total incident solar power:
   P_total = 1 kW m⁻² × 0.64 m² = 1000 W m⁻² × 0.64 m² = 640 W

2. Useful heating power absorbed (55% efficiency):
   P_useful = 640 W × 55% = 352 W (J s⁻¹)

3. Time required in seconds:
   t = E / P_useful = (6.3 × 10⁵ J) / 352 W ≈ 1789.77 s

4. Convert time into minutes:
   Time = 1789.77 / 60 ≈ 29.8 minutes (accept 29.8 ~ 30 mins)

b) The water is not as hot as expected. State ONE possible reason. [1 mark]

(b) 水溫沒有預期中那麼熱。試提出一個可能的原因。[1分]
Any ONE of the following reasons:
1. Heat energy was lost from the water, pipes, or bathtub to the surroundings.
2. Some thermal energy was absorbed by the water pipes or the bathtub container itself.
3. Sunlight intensity was lower than 1 kW m⁻² (due to cloud cover, low sun angle, or dust on the panel).
接受以下任一答案
1. 水、管道或浴缸散失了熱量到周圍環境中。
2. 部分熱能被水管或浴缸本身吸收，而非全部進入水中。
3. 實際光照強度低於 1 kW m⁻²（如遇雲層遮擋、太陽入射角度傾斜或面板積塵）。
Question 6 (Calculation)
3 Marks
An electric kettle has consumed 7.2 × 105 J in 12 minutes. What is the power of the kettle?
題目：一個電熱水煲在 12 分鐘內消耗了 7.2 × 105 J 的能量。電熱水煲的功率是多少？
• Energy consumed (E) = 7.2 × 10⁵ J
• Time in seconds (t) = 12 min = 12 × 60 s = 720 s
• Power (P) = E / t
  P = (7.2 × 10⁵ J) / 720 s = 1000 W (or 1 kW)
解析 代入公式 P = E / t 前，必須將 12 分鐘化為標準秒數 (12 × 60 = 720 s)，計算得出功率為 1000 W (或 1 kW)。
Question 7 (Calculation)
3 Marks
An immersion heater of power 100 W is used to heat 0.3 kg of a liquid with specific heat capacity 2000 J kg-1 °C-1. If the initial temperature of the liquid is 23 °C, find its temperature after 2 minutes. Assume all energy is absorbed by the liquid.
題目：一個功率為 100 W 的浸入式電熱器用來加熱 0.3 kg 比熱容量為 2000 J kg-1 °C-1 的液體。若該液體的初溫是 23 °C，求加熱 2 分鐘後的溫度。假設所有能量都被液體吸收。
1. Total energy supplied by heater:
   t = 2 min = 2 × 60 s = 120 s
   E = P × t = 100 W × 120 s = 12,000 J

2. Rise in temperature (ΔT):
   E = m · c · ΔT
   12000 = 0.3 × 2000 × ΔT
   12000 = 600 × ΔT
   ΔT = 20 °C

3. Final temperature (T_final):
   T_final = T_initial + ΔT = 23 °C + 20 °C = 43 °C
解析 初溫為 23 °C，計算出溫升 ΔT = 20 °C 後，切記相加得到加熱後的末溫：23 + 20 = 43 °C。
