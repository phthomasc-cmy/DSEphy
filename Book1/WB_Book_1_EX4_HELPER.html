Question 1 (P.55 Example 2.1)

Two electric kettles A and B have consumed 6.0 × 10⁵ J and 4.8 × 10⁵ J in 5 minutes respectively. The kettles are filled with the same mass of water at 25 °C.
Step 1 (a) Calculate the power of Kettle A

Convert time into seconds and use P = E / t.

Power of A = W (or J/s)
提示：時間需轉換成秒 (s)：5 分鐘 = 5 × 60 = 300 秒。
使用功率公式：P = E / t = 600,000 J / 300 s。
Step 2 (a) Calculate the power of Kettle B
Power of B = W (or J/s)
提示：P = E / t = 4.8 × 10⁵ J / 300 s。
Step 3 (b) Which kettle of water will boil first?
提示：兩水煲中水質量相同、初溫相同，加熱到沸騰所需能量相同。功率較大者加熱速率更快。

Question 2 (P.55 Example 2.2)

A solar water heating system has a solar panel of area 0.5 m². The panel captures 25% of the solar energy it receives, and uses the energy to heat water. Assume sunlight delivers energy onto the Earth's surface at 1 kW m⁻².
If a bathtub of water is heated for 40 minutes, how much energy is consumed for the heating process?
Step 1 Find the effective heating power of the panel

Total power received = Area × Solar intensity. Only 25% is captured.

Effective Power = W
提示：1 kW m⁻² = 1000 W m⁻²。
接收到的太陽總功率 = 0.5 m² × 1000 W m⁻² = 500 W。
有效加熱功率 = 500 W × 25%。
Step 2 Calculate total energy consumed (E = Pt)
Energy E = J
提示：加熱時間 t = 40 分鐘 = 40 × 60 = 2400 秒。
能量 E = 有效功率 × 時間 = 125 W × 2400 s。

Question 3 (P.59 Example 2.3)

A pot of tea is supplied with 30 kJ of heat. The heat capacity of the tea is 7500 J °C⁻¹.
Step 1 (a) Find the increase in temperature of the tea (ΔT)
ΔT = °C
提示：注意單位轉換，30 kJ = 30,000 J。
熱容量公式：E = C ΔT => ΔT = E / C = 30000 / 7500。
Step 2 (b) Determine the increase in internal energy when temp rises from 25 °C to 80 °C
Energy Increase = kJ
提示：溫度變化 ΔT = 80 - 25 = 55 °C。
內能增加量 E = C ΔT = 7500 × 55 J。最後除以 1000 換算成 kJ。

Question 4 (P.62 Example 2.4)

A wok of 700 g oil is heated with 2.1 × 10⁵ J of energy at 25 °C. If the specific heat capacity of the oil is 2 kJ kg⁻¹ °C⁻¹, what temperature is the oil heated to?
Step 1 Calculate the temperature rise (ΔT)
ΔT = °C
提示：注意單位換算！
質量 m = 700 g = 0.7 kg。
比熱容量 c = 2 kJ kg⁻¹ °C⁻¹ = 2000 J kg⁻¹ °C⁻¹。
公式：E = mcΔT => ΔT = E / (mc) = 210,000 / (0.7 × 2000)。
Step 2 Find the final temperature (T)
Final Temperature T = °C
提示：最終溫度 T = 初溫 + 升高的溫度 = 25 °C + ΔT。

Question 5 (P.63 Example 2.5)

Francis is heating 0.8 kg of water from 25 °C to 100 °C. The kettle to be used requires 240 J of energy to raise its temperature by 1 °C (C_kettle = 240 J °C⁻¹). Take c_water = 4200 J kg⁻¹ °C⁻¹.
Step 1 (a) Increase in internal energy of the kettle of water

Both water and kettle are heated together from 25 °C to 100 °C (ΔT = 75 °C).

Internal Energy Increase = J
提示：E = (m_water × c_water + C_kettle) × ΔT
= (0.8 × 4200 + 240) × (100 - 25) = (3360 + 240) × 75 = 3600 × 75 J。
Step 2 (b) Total energy required assuming 10% is lost to surroundings
Total Energy = J
提示：若損耗 10%，則有效吸收的能量佔總能量的 90% (1 - 10%)。
總能量 E_total = E / (1 - 0.10) = 270,000 / 0.9。
Step 3 (c) If the water boils in 2 minutes, what is the power of the kettle?
Power = W
提示：時間 t = 2 分鐘 = 120 秒。
P = E_total / t = 300,000 J / 120 s。

Question 6 (P.66 Example 2.6)

A student conducts Experiment 2.1 to find the specific heat capacity of water with the following data:
• Mass of water = 220 g
• Initial reading of joulemeter = 32 050 J
• Final reading of joulemeter = 58 635 J
• Initial temperature = 25 °C, Final temperature = 55 °C
Step 1 (a) Calculate the specific heat capacity of water
c = J kg⁻¹ °C⁻¹
提示：
供給能量 E = 58 635 - 32 050 = 26 585 J。
質量 m = 220 g = 0.22 kg。
升溫 ΔT = 55 - 25 = 30 °C。
公式：c = E / (m ΔT) = 26585 / (0.22 × 30)。
Step 2 (b) If the student mistakenly includes both the water and beaker in the mass:

Would the experimental value of c be larger or smaller than standard? Why?

提示：根據 c = E / (m ΔT)，如果測量質量時包含了燒杯，所記錄的質量 m 就被高估了 (overestimated)。因為 m 在分母，計算出的 c 值就會偏小 (underestimated)。

Question 7 (P.66 Example 2.7)

A housewife fills a rice cooker with 2 kg of 15 °C water, and switches it on for 10 min. The final temperature of the water is 85 °C. Take c_water = 4.2 kJ kg⁻¹ °C⁻¹.
Step 1 (a) What is the useful output power of the cooker?
Useful Power P = W
提示：時間 t = 10 min = 600 s。
比熱 c = 4.2 kJ kg⁻¹ °C⁻¹ = 4200 J kg⁻¹ °C⁻¹。
溫差 ΔT = 85 - 15 = 70 °C。
P = mcΔT / t = (2 × 4200 × 70) / 600。
Step 2 (b) Heat 2 kg congee from 25 °C to 55 °C (c = 4.9 kJ kg⁻¹ °C⁻¹) at the same power

Calculate the time needed for this process.

Time needed = seconds (s)
提示：使用第一步算出的輸出功率 P = 980 W。
粥升溫需要的能量 E = mcΔT = 2 × 4900 × (55 - 25) = 294,000 J。
時間 t = E / P = 294,000 / 980 秒 (即 5 分鐘)。
