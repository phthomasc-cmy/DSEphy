Thermal Absorption, Emission & Cooling

Turn the lamp ON to radiate heat. The dull black surface absorbs radiation readily, while the shiny surface reflects it. Turn the lamp OFF to observe cooling: the dull black block rapidly radiates away heat (ε ≈ 0.95), while the shiny block retains it (ε ≈ 0.10).
