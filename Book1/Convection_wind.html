🌬️ Uneven Heating & Wind Production

"Some parts of the earth's surface heats up more quickly than the other. The uneven temperature causes convection currents to occur in the atmosphere. In this way, wind is produced."
Key Mechanics
Warm, Low Density (Rising / Low Pressure)
Cool, High Density (Sinking / High Pressure)
Surface Wind = Movement from High to Low Pressure
