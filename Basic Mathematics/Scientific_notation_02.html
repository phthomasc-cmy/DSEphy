Topic: Scientific Notation, Standard Form & Physics Quantities

Instructions: Express all final answers in standard scientific notation format: a × 10b, where 1 ≤ |a| < 10. Round the mantissa (a) to 3 significant figures unless specified otherwise.
Electromagnetism
Q1. Fundamental Charge

The magnitude of the charge of a single electron is approximately 0.0000000000000000001602 C. Express this in scientific notation.

× 10 C
Correct Answer: 1.60 × 10-19 C
Astrophysics / Gravitation
Q2. Earth's Mass

The mass of the Earth is measured to be 5,970,000,000,000,000,000,000,000 kg. Convert this quantity to standard scientific notation (3 sig. fig.).

× 10 kg
Correct Answer: 5.97 × 1024 kg
Wave Motion (Optics)
Q3. Wavelength of Red Light

A helium-neon laser emits red light with a wavelength of 632.8 nm (nanometers). Convert this value into meters (m) and express it in scientific notation (3 sig. fig.).
(Hint: 1 nm = 10-9 m)

× 10 m
Correct Answer: 6.33 × 10-7 m (632.8 × 10-9 m = 6.328 × 10-7 m ≈ 6.33 × 10-7 m)
Atomic & Nuclear Physics
Q4. Energy of a Photon (Calculation)

The energy E of a photon is given by E = h f.
Calculate E if Planck's constant h = 6.63 × 10-34 J s and frequency f = 7.50 × 1014 Hz. Give your answer in scientific notation (3 sig. fig.).

× 10 J
Correct Answer: 4.97 × 10-19 J
Workings: (6.63 × 7.50) × 10-34 + 14 = 49.725 × 10-20 = 4.9725 × 10-19 J ≈ 4.97 × 10-19 J
Electricity
Q5. Number of Electrons Transferred

A current of 2.00 A flows through a circuit for 4.00 minutes. Calculate the total number of electrons that passed through the circuit.
(Charge Q = I × t, e = 1.60 × 10-19 C)

× 10 electrons
Correct Answer: 3.00 × 1021 electrons
Workings:
t = 4.00 × 60 = 240 s
Q = 2.00 × 240 = 480 C
N = Q / e = 480 / (1.60 × 10-19) = 3.00 × 1021
