Part 1: Converting Prefixes to Standard SI Units
Electronics
Q1. Capacitance in Microfarads

A capacitor has a rating of 4.7 μF (microfarads). Express this capacitance in standard SI units (farads, F) using scientific notation.

× 10 F
Correct Answer: 4.70 × 10-6 F
Note: micro (μ) = 10-6. So 4.7 μF = 4.70 × 10-6 F.
Power Generation
Q2. Power Station Output

A wind turbine generates 2.5 MW (megawatts) of electrical power. Convert this power into watts (W) in scientific notation.

× 10 W
Correct Answer: 2.50 × 106 W
Note: Mega (M) = 106. So 2.5 MW = 2.50 × 106 W.
Atomic Scale
Q3. Atomic Radius

The radius of a silicon atom is 110 pm (picometers). Express this distance in meters (m) in standard scientific notation.

× 10 m
Correct Answer: 1.10 × 10-10 m
Workings: 110 pm = 110 × 10-12 m = 1.10 × 10-10 m.
Part 2: Multi-Step Physics Calculations
Circuits (Ohm's Law)
Q4. Electric Current

A potential difference of 12.0 V is applied across a 6.80 kΩ resistor. Calculate the current I flowing through the circuit in amperes (A).
(Formula: I = V / R)

× 10 A
Correct Answer: 1.76 × 10-3 A
Workings:
R = 6.80 kΩ = 6.80 × 103 Ω
I = 12.0 / (6.80 × 103) = 0.0017647... A ≈ 1.76 × 10-3 A
Wave Motion
Q5. Light Wave Frequency

A green laser emits light with a wavelength of 532 nm. Given the speed of light c = 3.00 × 108 m s-1, calculate the frequency f of the wave.
(Formula: c = f × λ)

× 10 Hz
Correct Answer: 5.64 × 1014 Hz
Workings:
λ = 532 nm = 532 × 10-9 m = 5.32 × 10-7 m
f = c / λ = (3.00 × 108) / (5.32 × 10-7) = 5.6391... × 1014 Hz ≈ 5.64 × 1014 Hz
Energy & Power
Q6. Pulsed Laser Energy

An ultra-fast research laser emits a power pulse of 1.50 GW (gigawatts) for a duration of 20.0 ns (nanoseconds). Calculate the total energy delivered in the pulse in Joules (J).
(Formula: E = P × t)

× 10 J
Correct Answer: 3.00 × 101 J (or 3.00 × 101 J)
Workings:
P = 1.50 × 109 W
t = 20.0 × 10-9 s
E = (1.50 × 109) × (20.0 × 10-9) = 30.0 J = 3.00 × 101 J
