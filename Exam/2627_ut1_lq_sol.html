1. Car Cooling System & Heat Transfer (4 marks)

(a) Why radiators are painted black [1 mark]
Step 1: Relate radiation property to heat emission rate Car radiators are usually painted in black so that heat can be lost by radiation at a higher rate.
(OR: Dull black surfaces are good emitters of thermal radiation.)
[1A]
啞黑色表面是極佳的熱輻射體（good emitter of radiation）。將散熱器塗成黑色，能使引擎傳導過來的熱量更快以「熱輻射」的形式散失到大氣中。
(b) Purpose of thin aluminium fins [2 marks]
Step 1: Geometric effect This design increases the total surface area of the aluminium tubes exposed to the air.
[1A]
Step 2: Effect on heat loss rate As a result, heat is transferred/lost to the surroundings at a higher rate.
[1A]
薄鋁鰭片（fins）大幅增加了金屬組件接觸周圍空氣的表面積。散熱速率與接觸表面積成正比，表面積越大，熱量透過傳導與對流散發到周圍環境的速度越快。
(c) Purpose of the fan [1 mark]
Step 1: Mechanism enhanced The fan increases the rate of heat loss through convection (by providing forced airflow).
[1A]
風扇轉動強制帶動空氣流動（強制對流），能加速將散熱器附近的暖空氣帶走並換入冷空氣，從而提高對流（convection）的散熱速率。

2. Thermometer Calibration & Physical Properties (6 marks)

(a) Feasibility of Device X as a Thermometer [2 marks]
Step 1: Direct statement Device \(X\) can be used for making a thermometer.
[1A]
Step 2: Scientific justification It is because property \(H\) depends on temperature \(T\) linearly (represented by a continuous straight line in graph A).
[1A]
作為測溫性質（thermometric property），該物理量必須隨溫度呈單調且**線性（linear）**變化。圖 A 中的性質 \(H\) 隨溫度呈直線下降，關係確定；而圖 B 中的性質 \(K\) 升溫後保持定值，無法反映溫度的持續改變。
(b)(i) Temperature Estimation for Freezer (H = 55 units) [2 marks]
Step 1: Set up calibration formula using ice point (\(0^\circ\text{C} \to 50\)) & steam point (\(100^\circ\text{C} \to 30\)) $$\text{Temperature } T = \frac{H - H_0}{H_{100} - H_0} \times 100^\circ\text{C} = \frac{55 - 50}{30 - 50} \times 100^\circ\text{C}$$
[1M]
Step 2: Calculate result $$T = \frac{5}{-20} \times 100 = \mathbf{-25^\circ\text{C}}$$
[1A]
冰點（\(0^\circ\text{C}\)）時 \(H=50\)；蒸汽點/沸點（\(100^\circ\text{C}\)）時 \(H=30\)。
按線性比例外推：\(T = \frac{55 - 50}{30 - 50} \times 100 = \frac{5}{-20} \times 100 = \mathbf{-25^\circ\text{C}}\)。
(b)(ii) Magnitude of H at 25 °C [2 marks]
Step 1: Substitute \(T = 25^\circ\text{C}\) into the calibration equation $$25 = \frac{H - 50}{30 - 50} \times 100$$
[1M]
Step 2: Solve algebraically for \(H\) $$\frac{25}{100} = \frac{H - 50}{-20} \implies -5 = H - 50 \implies \mathbf{H = 45\text{ units}}$$
[1A]
代入 \(25^\circ\text{C}\)：\(25 = \frac{H - 50}{-20} \times 100\)，兩邊同除以 100 得 \(0.25 \times (-20) = H - 50\)，解得 \(H - 50 = -5 \implies \mathbf{H = 45\text{ 單位}}\)。

3. Household Fan Heater & Thermodynamics (10 marks)

(a) Why the heater should be placed on the ground [2 marks]
Step 1: Density variation and movement of air Hot air is less dense than cold air, so hot air rises while cold air sinks.
[1A]
Step 2: Formation of convection current Thus, an effective convection current can be set up in the room to circulate heat evenly.
[1A]
熱空氣受熱膨脹、密度小於冷空氣而上升；上層較冷的空氣密度較大下沉，從而在房間內形成對流循環（convection current），將熱量均勻擴散至全屋。
(b) Estimate time needed to reach 20 °C [4 marks]
Step 1: Calculate mass of air $$m = \rho V = 1.225 \times 60 = 73.5\text{ kg}$$
[1M]
Step 2: Calculate heat energy required (\(E = mc\Delta T\)) $$E = 73.5 \times 1000 \times (20 - 12) = 5.88 \times 10^5\text{ J}$$
[1M]
Step 3: Relate energy to power (\(E = Pt\)) $$5.88 \times 10^5 = 2000 \times t$$
[1M]
Step 4: Solve for time \(t\) $$t = \frac{5.88 \times 10^5}{2000} = \mathbf{294\text{ s}}$$
[1A]
① 空氣質量：\(m = 1.225 \times 60 = 73.5\text{ kg}\)
② 所需熱量：\(\Delta T = 20 - 12 = 8^\circ\text{C}\)，\(E = mc\Delta T = 73.5 \times 1000 \times 8 = 5.88 \times 10^5\text{ J}\)
③ 加熱時間：\(E = Pt \implies t = \frac{5.88 \times 10^5}{2000} = \mathbf{294\text{ 秒}}\)。
(c)(i) Why actual time differs from result in (b) [1 mark]
Step 1: State heat loss In reality, there is heat lost to the cooler environment outside Joyce’s room (e.g., through walls and windows).
[1A]
理論計算假設沒有任何熱量損失，但現實中房間會持續透過牆壁、窗戶及門縫向外界較冷的環境散失熱量。
(c)(ii) Why temperature becomes steady at 400 s [1 mark]
Step 1: Thermal equilibrium state The rate of heat given out by the heater is balanced by the rate of heat lost to the environment outside.
[1A]
隨著室內外溫差加大，散熱速率也隨之增加。在 400 秒時，散熱速率恰好等於暖風機的供熱功率（2000 W），熱量收支平衡，溫度不再上升。
(c)(iii) Comment on Joyce's claim for a larger room [2 marks]
Step 1: Judgment Joyce is wrong / incorrect. The larger room cannot reach 20 °C no matter how long the heater is switched on.
[1A]
Step 2: Justification A larger room has larger boundary surface area (walls/windows), resulting in a higher rate of heat loss. It balances the 2000 W heater output at a temperature below 20 °C.
[1A]
Joyce 的看法是錯誤的。更大的房間擁有更大的外牆和窗戶表面積，散熱速率大幅增加。供熱功率（2000 W）在尚未達到 20 °C 時就會與散熱速率平衡，因此無論開多久都無法升至 20 °C。

4. Measuring Specific Heat Capacity of a Metal Block (9 marks)

(a) Why drops of oil are added into the holes [1 mark]
Step 1: Thermal contact enhancement To ensure good thermal contact between the heater, the thermometer, and the metal block (by expelling air gaps).
[1A]
空氣是熱的不良導體。在金屬孔內滴入油是為了排擠空氣，確保電熱器、溫度計與金屬塊之間有良好的熱接觸（good thermal contact）。
(b) When to record the final temperature [3 marks]
Step 1: Clear judgment No, the final temperature should not be recorded immediately after turning off the heater.
[1A]
Step 2: Correct measurement procedure The highest temperature obtained after turning off the heater should be recorded as the final temperature.
[1A]
Step 3: Thermodynamic justification Because the immersion heater remains hotter than the block just after power-off and continues transferring heat.
[1A]
不應該立即記錄。剛切斷電源時，發熱絲的溫度依然高於金屬塊，餘熱會繼續傳入金屬塊中。因此應觀察並記錄關閉電源後溫度計所上升到的最高讀數。
(c) Calculation of specific heat capacity [2 marks]
Step 1: Energy balance formula \(E = mc\Delta T\) $$\Delta E = 35219 - 12513 = 22706\text{ J}, \quad \Delta T = 48 - 26 = 22^\circ\text{C}$$ $$22706 = 3 \times c \times 22$$
[1M]
Step 2: Evaluate result with units $$c = \frac{22706}{66} \approx \mathbf{344\text{ J kg}^{-1}\ ^\circ\text{C}^{-1}}$$
[1A]
電能輸入：\(\Delta E = 35219 - 12513 = 22706\text{ J}\)
金屬塊溫升：\(\Delta T = 48 - 26 = 22^\circ\text{C}\)
代入 \(E = mc\Delta T\)：\(22706 = 3 \times c \times 22 \implies \mathbf{c = 344\text{ J kg}^{-1}\ ^\circ\text{C}^{-1}}\)。
(d) Experimental error if cotton wool is omitted [3 marks]
Step 1: Direction of error The calculated specific heat capacity will be larger than the actual value.
[1A]
Step 2: Physical mechanism Without cotton wool insulation, energy loss to the surroundings becomes significant.
[1A]
Step 3: Mathematical reasoning More electrical energy \(E\) is required to heat up the block (or observed temperature change \(\Delta T\) is smaller), inflating \(c = \frac{E}{m\Delta T}\).
[1A]
實驗結果將高於真實值。金屬塊未包裹保溫棉時，散失到周圍環境的熱量大幅增加，導致相同能量輸入下的溫升 \(\Delta T\) 偏小（或需要更多電能才能達到相同溫升）。由公式 \(c = \frac{E}{m\Delta T}\) 可知，計算出的比熱容必偏大。

5. Phase Change & Freezing of Water (7 marks)

(a) Time required for boiling water to freeze completely [4 marks]
Step 1: Two-stage heat release formula (Cooling + Freezing) $$E = mc\Delta T + m\ell_f$$
[1M + 1M]
Step 2: Substitute given values $$E = 0.1 \times 4200 \times (100 - 0) + 0.1 \times 3.34 \times 10^5$$ $$E = 42000 + 33400 = 75400\text{ J}$$
—
Step 3: Relate to energy removal rate (500 J/min) $$t = \frac{75400}{500}$$
[1M]
Step 4: State final time $$t = \mathbf{151\text{ min}} \quad (\text{or } 150.8\text{ min})$$
[1A]
放熱過程分為兩個階段：
① 熱水從 \(100^\circ\text{C}\) 降溫至 \(0^\circ\text{C}\)：\(E_1 = mc\Delta T = 0.1 \times 4200 \times 100 = 42\,000\text{ J}\)
② \(0^\circ\text{C}\) 的水凝固成 \(0^\circ\text{C}\) 的冰：\(E_2 = m\ell_f = 0.1 \times 3.34 \times 10^5 = 33\,400\text{ J}\)
總放熱量：\(E = 42000 + 33400 = 75\,400\text{ J}\)
所需時間：雪櫃每分鐘抽走 500 J，\(t = \frac{75400}{500} = \mathbf{151\text{ 分鐘}}\)。
(b) Why the real-world freezing time is shorter [3 marks]
Reason 1: Mass reduction due to evaporation As some hot water evaporates into steam/vapor during cooling, there is less than 100 g of water remaining in the glass to be frozen.
[1A + 1A]
Reason 2: Latent heat removal via evaporation Evaporation carries away latent heat of vaporization, producing a strong cooling effect that accelerates temperature drop.
[1A]
實際結冰時間縮短的兩大關鍵原因：
① 水分蒸發導致質量減少：部分沸水在冷卻過程中蒸發為水蒸氣逸出，杯中剩餘需結冰的水少於 100 g，總放熱需求減少。
② 蒸發產生冷卻效應（cooling effect）：水分子蒸發時會帶走大量的汽化潛熱，進一步加速了熱水的冷卻降溫過程。
