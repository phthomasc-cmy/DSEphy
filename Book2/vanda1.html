Stage 1 (0 s ≤ t < 10 s): Moving Right & Slowing Down
The car moves to the right (v > 0), but acceleration points left (a = −1 m s−2). Since velocity and acceleration have opposite signs, the speed decreases by 1 m s−1 every second.
Stage 2 (t = 10 s): Momentarily at Rest
Velocity drops to exactly v = 0 m s−1 at displacement s = +50 m. The car instantaneously stops before reversing direction. (Acceleration is still −1 m s−2!)
Stage 3 (10 s < t ≤ 20 s): Moving Left & Speeding Up
The car moves to the left (v < 0). Since velocity and acceleration now have the same sign (both negative), the car accelerates leftward, speeding up to −10 m s−1.
Rule: When velocity and acceleration have opposite directions, the object slows down (decelerates). When they share the same direction, the object speeds up.
