2D Vector Addition: Tip-to-Tail Method

For vectors in a 2D plane, we combine them using the tip-to-tail method, Pythagoras' theorem, and compass bearings.

🧭 Compass Bearing Rule: Angles are measured starting from either North (N) or South (S) towards East (E) or West (W) (e.g., $\text{N}60^\circ\text{E}$ or $\text{S}30^\circ\text{W}$).
